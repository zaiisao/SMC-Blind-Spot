## 1. The exchange with the professor

### What was asked
Two scenarios raised: (1) the first beat is not a downbeat (anacrusis), (2) there is a
stretch from the first frame to the first downbeat. Proposal: predict a **global latent for
the offset of the first bar's start**.

### The professor's position
- Beat-tracking DBNs are designed to emit a beat sequence *regardless* of whether the song
  starts on a downbeat, so no such variable is needed.
- Asked whether a `bar_k` (bar **index**) had been added, distinct from bar phase `phi_k`.
- Asked whether frame-wise downbeat/beat/nonbeat posteriors weren't sufficient.
- Later **conceded** the frame-wise question was his own slip, and accepted that the real
  question was how `z_t` behaves where no beat has appeared — answering: weak observations
  mean `z_t` is uncertain, and the uncertainty should be carried as-is.
- Redirected to a new task: find or derive **phrase-level** labels (the "dancer starts
  moving at the main phrase" intuition).

### Assessment given
- He validated the **question**, never the **solution**. His own answer (carry the
  uncertainty) is the opposite move from adding a latent.
- Scenarios (1) and (2) are not independent — both are the same `phi_1` initial condition.
- The framing that survives his objections: this is a **variational inference** question,
  not a beat-tracking one. A classical DBN puts a uniform over the initial bar position and
  Viterbi resolves it exactly, for free. An amortized `q` must produce it from a network,
  and that is where it breaks. `k` with `p(k) = Uniform` is not an addition to the
  formalism; it is the DBN's own initial state, restored.
- Weak points in the replies sent: "emission **infers** probabilities" (emission *defines*
  the likelihood; only `q(z|x)` infers); "z where no beat has appeared" (there is no such
  region — `phi_t` exists at every frame; it is an evidence-poor region where the prior
  carries the posterior); and the professor's numbers request was never answered with data.
- The sharper unasked question: in a lead-in with no annotated downbeats, is the recon term
  **masked** or used as **negative evidence**? Under (b) the emission actively pulls the
  anchor. That is the edge case worth raising.

### On phrase level
Would be a **new rung** (phrase phase + bars-per-phrase + its own initial condition), not a
new kind of variable. Correction issued: phrase boundaries are downbeat-aligned, so they
would **not** break the residual half-bar/backbeat flips, which are sub-bar. Phrase buys a
long-range prior and meter-change handling, neither of which is the current bottleneck.

## 2. Design thread: the anchor

### What the project already had
- `anchor_k` v2: 64 **phase slots per bar**, exact marginalisation, phase-binned histogram
  of the frontend's own beat/downbeat channels, rotation augmentation.
- `anchor_time` (uncommitted, unrun): candidates on a **time grid**, stride 4 frames = 80 ms
  = 12.5/s (14.3/s is the ideal derived from tolerance; 3.5 frames is not an integer, and
  integer frames are what keep the anchor value *read* rather than interpolated). Its own
  evidence head — a linear on the 512 features, randomly initialised on purpose — plus
  8 Fourier harmonics, standardised across candidates.

### The closed form
```
shift = -arg( sum_t a_t * e^{i*mu_t} )        # circular mean of the evidence, folded under mu
phi_t = wrap(mu_t + shift)                     # one constant added to every frame
```
`a_t` = per-frame "downbeat-ness"; `mu_t` = the model's phase. Each frame contributes an
arrow of length `a_t` pointing at `mu_t`; the sum's **angle** is where the audio's downbeats
sit in the model's own coordinate, and its **length** measures agreement across bars.

Points settled in discussion:
- Deployment is deterministic (`infer_phase` returns `mu`, `@torch.no_grad`); the von Mises
  sample appears only in the training `forward`. The read-out swap is **not** mean-vs-sample.
- The normalised resultant `|R| / sum_t a_t` is the right confidence (raw `|R|` is
  scale-dependent). Confounded by peak width and the activation floor.
- **No auxiliary loss is needed.** `q`'s mean may be any function of `x`, so the closed form
  is a reparameterisation of `q`, not a new term. Deleting the learned head also deletes the
  categorical, the `log C - H[q]` term, the augmentation, and the `[B, C, T]` tensor.
- Gauge: if both `mu` and `k` can rotate the trajectory, only the sum is identified (v2's
  offset head measurably abdicated to 459 ms). Fix: make the ramp anchor-free, `mu_1 = 0`.
- Activations are a frame-wise **linear** of the 512 features, so an own-evidence linear can
  represent them exactly; only the initialisation differs.

All on **synthetic ideal features** unless stated: Gaussian bumps at true downbeats,
broadcast to 512 channels, 45 s windows, periods Uniform(2,5) s, constant within a song.

### 3.3 Rate identifiability (no training; grid the objective, oracle anchor)

FP cost is **rate-invariant**: 133.6 / 133.8 / 134.0 / 134.1 at 1x / 2x / 3x / 4x.
TP cost near-identical at integer multiples: 75.7 / 76.6 / 77.5 / 78.3.

Preference for the true rate over 2x: **1.02 nats** (pw 1), 4.47 (pw 5), 26.05 (pw 30),
against a recon of ~209. Sub-multiples and non-integer ratios: **-50 to -1417 nats**.

Per-song argmax: true rate wins **31–34%**; 2x 25%, 3x 14%, 4x 14–19%; **/2 and /3: 0.0%**.

Not fixable by emission shape: sharpness `b` 1.3 → 40 gives 34% → 40%; a width-parameterised
von Mises emission (`ke` 2…1000, a knob the triangle lacks) gives 34% → **20%**.

**And the prior is exactly indifferent too.** `kl_jitter` penalises `cos(accel)`,
the *second* difference, so every constant rate is equally probable a priori.

| fix | true-rate argmax |

| log-normal tempo prior, sigma 0.6 / 0.4 / 0.25 / 0.15 | 40.6 / 50.0 / 63.5 / **77.1%** |

The tempo prior at sigma 0.15 is mis-specified (corpus is Uniform(2,5) s) and fights
`pos_weight` (77% at pw 1 → 53% at pw 5). The per-event penalty collapses above its window
(lambda 10 at pw 1 → median 0.20x the true rate).

## 4. Where it stands

**Two bottlenecks, and they are coupled.**

1. **Rate is not identified.** The likelihood is near-indifferent across integer multiples
   (~1 nat); the prior is exactly indifferent; the clamp is absorbing. Three candidate fixes
   score 100% on the landscape; the one tried in training was inert.
2. **The learned anchor head fails anyway.** 15–17% within 70 ms given a perfect rate,
   against 100% (ideal evidence) or F 0.50 (own trained evidence) for the zero-parameter
   closed form.

**Neither is solved end to end.** Landscape geometry is not training dynamics — the trap
`probe-training-disconnect` warns about, walked into once tonight.

**Order of operations correction:** they cannot be fixed one at a time. Nothing about the
rate is evaluable while `agree = 0.0%`, because a wrong anchor leaves the reconstruction
unable to prefer any rate.

### Next experiments
1. Replace the hard `clamp()` on the rate with a bounded smooth transform — nothing else can
   act until a railed rate can return.
2. Closed-form anchor **and** a rate term in the **same** run.
3. Recompute the halving cost against the model's own evidence `a_t` instead of `y`, on
   **real** audio (on synthetic it passes tautologically).
4. Stress-test the asymmetry rule against missing downbeats (drop 20–40%) — the one regime
   where "slowest that doesn't collapse" is expected to fail.

## 5. Corrections issued during the session

- The F 0.468 → 0.752 jump: **82% is the deployment read-out**, 18% the learned head,
  ~0.002 co-adaptation. Not "the exact-marginalised k".
- Enumeration was **not** the mechanism; **representation** was (phase-folded evidence:
  206 ms → 38 ms with the same atan2 output).
- The anchor landscape is **flat-topped unimodal** over phase within a bar, and **periodic**
  over anchor times across a window. Both true; different domains.
- A conditional prior `p(phi_dot|x)` with free capacity would likely **not** identify
  anything — it can adapt to agree with `q` and the KL vanishes. Rigidity is the point.
- Batch 16 would **not** halve wall time; frontend cost is per-window.
- The event penalty was **not** an "accelerant" — the control collapses identically.
- The rail direction is **not stable** (lr 1e-3 → high rail, lr 3e-4 → low rail).
